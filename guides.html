01 · THE BASICS

Your first note

Talk the way it comes out. Messy is fine, that's my job.

  1. Tap the + in the middle of the tab bar. A sheet asks What do you want to capture?
  2. Pick one: Talk (any language, even two in one sentence), Type, Photo a page, Import audio or Record a meeting.
  3. For a voice note, just talk, then stop. A few seconds later the note is ready, organized.
FasterHold the plus to start a voice note at once. Lock the screen or switch apps while you talk: the recording carries on.

02 · WHAT YOU GET

What a note becomes

One ramble in, a clean page out.

  1. A title, a short summary and the key points.
  2. Actions with the dates you said out loud. Tap When or the bell on an action to set or change its reminder.
  3. Promises go both ways, with names: what you owe lands in You promised, what Sara or Omar owe you lands in Others promised.
  4. Your own words stay under the summary in What you said, untouched, so you can always check.
TipTap the + next to a key point to make it an action, or Add an action to write one yourself.

03 · TODAY

Today and Noto's jar

The Today tab is your day: Today's actions, what you promised, and my corner of the room.

  1. Tick an action when it's done. A paper star flies into my jar.
  2. Keep a promise to someone and it's a gold star, with a promise kept card.
  3. Want to tell them? Share this shares that card only, nothing else from your notes.
Good to knowPrefer less movement? Settings has Calm mode, and it turns on by itself in meetings.

04 · ASK

Ask your notes

Everything you've said becomes something you can ask, in any language.

  1. On Today, tap the sparkle at the top. You can also type a question in the search on the Notes tab.
  2. Ask it like you'd ask a person: What did I promise Omar?
  3. The answer names the notes it came from. Tap one to read the original.
TipAnswers come from your own notes only.

05 · MEETINGS

Meetings

Put the phone on the table. No bot, no invite, no link.

  1. Open the Meetings tab and tap Start a meeting. Add a title and who's joining if you like; both are optional.
  2. Choose what you get, then tap Start meeting.
  3. Pause or end it from the screen, the lock screen or the Dynamic Island. Mark this moment pins a point you want to find later.
  4. You get a notification when your minutes are ready: speakers named, decisions, open questions and actions for each person.
Transcript, speakers and recapIn the cloud. Mixed languages, the speakers told apart, and the minutes. Uses cloud minutes.
Transcript on this iPhoneFree. Nothing is uploaded. No speakers and no recap.
Then ask itInside a meeting, Ask about this meeting asks that one meeting on its own. How many cloud minutes each plan has is on the Plans page.

06 · PAPER

Photo a page

A whiteboard, a handout, a screenshot of a long chat.

  1. Tap +, then Photo a page. Snap or pick up to five pages or screenshots.
  2. I read every page and write it up like any other note.
  3. Anything asked of you becomes an action, with its date when one is written there.

07 · FROM OTHER APPS

Bring in voice notes and more

The seven minute voice note from a friend, readable in seconds.

  1. In WhatsApp, Telegram, Mail, Files, Photos or any app, tap Share on a voice note, a piece of text or up to five pictures.
  2. Pick NotoMix in the share sheet.
  3. It becomes a note like your own: summary, key points and actions.
Or from inside the appTap +, then Import audio to pick an audio file yourself.

08 · SHARING

Share a note

Send the parts that matter, as text, a picture or a link.

  1. Open a note and tap the share button at the top. Share this note opens.
  2. Tick what goes. Summary, key points, actions, the transcript, a card picture, the recording, or a link.
  3. Send it with Message or Mail, Copy it, or tap More for any other app.
About the linkIt opens in any browser, no app needed. The note travels inside the link itself, it is never stored on a server, ours included. Anyone who has NotoMix can import it with one tap.

09 · LANGUAGES

Two languages, one note

Arabic and English in the same sentence is how a lot of us talk. Talk that way.

  1. Just record. Switch languages mid sentence whenever you like.
  2. Want every note written in one language? Settings, Organize in my language. Notes are written in the app's language, whatever you speak.
  3. When a note was turned into your language, a ⇄ chip at the top says which language you spoke. Tap it to see the note the way you said it, and tap again to go back.
TipWhat you said always keeps your exact words, in the languages you said them.

10 · MORNINGS

Morning Brief

One notification every morning: your actions, what's due, and your day.

  1. On Today, tap your picture at the top, then Settings.
  2. Under Morning Brief, set the Brief time you want.
  3. Turn on Include calendar to see your meetings in it too. Your calendar is read on your iPhone only.

11 · WITHOUT OPENING THE APP

Siri, widgets and the lock screen

The quickest way in is the one already on your screen.

  1. Siri: Record with NotoMix Stop recording in NotoMix Start a meeting in NotoMix What did I promise today in NotoMix Ask NotoMix
  2. Action Button (iPhone 15 Pro and later): Settings, Action Button, Shortcut, then NotoMix and Record.
  3. Widgets: Your day (medium) shows what's due and lets you tick it off. Quick Record goes on the Home Screen (small) or under the clock on the lock screen.
  4. Control Center (iOS 18): add the Record a note control.
  5. While you record: the lock screen and the Dynamic Island show it, with Pause and End.
TipSiri knows the app by the name under its icon on your Home Screen, so say that name in the phrase.

12 · TALK IN ANY APP

The NotoMix keyboard

Dictate into any text box, and I clean up the ums.

  1. Open the iPhone Settings app: General, Keyboard, Keyboards, Add New Keyboard, then NotoMix.
  2. Tap NotoMix in that list and turn on Allow Full Access. It is only for the microphone: nothing you type is kept or sent.
  3. In any app, switch to the NotoMix keyboard with the globe, and tap the microphone.
  4. NotoMix opens to listen. Go back to where you were typing with the arrow at the top left, and talk. Your words are written into that app.
TipTurn on Save as a note too and what you dictate also becomes a note, actions and all. After that I stay ready for three minutes, so the next time the keyboard starts me itself.

13 · ON YOUR WRIST

Apple Watch

Record on your wrist, even with the phone at home.

  1. Open NotoMix on the watch. It starts recording right away.
  2. Tap Stop and send.
  3. It goes to your iPhone when it's nearby and is organized there, like any other note.

14 · LOOKING BACK

Insights

What you captured, what you finished and the promises you kept.

  1. Open the Insights tab and pick Day, Week, Month or Year.
  2. See your day hour by hour, your chattiest day, and who promised you what.
  3. Omar still owes you something? Send a nudge opens your share sheet with a friendly message. I never send it myself.
Good to knowEvery number here is counted on your iPhone.

15 · YOUR STUFF

Your account and your notes

Your notes live on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.

  1. Your first note needs no account. Sign in or create one whenever you want, from your picture at the top of Today.
  2. With iCloud on, your notes are backed up there. Settings shows when the last backup was, and audio and photo backups are yours to switch on.
  3. To leave, Settings, Delete account. It removes your account and your notes from this device and your iCloud.
PlansThe free plan and what Pro adds are on the Plans page, with the price for your country.
