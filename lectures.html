Before you download

How do I summarize a lecture with AI?

Record the lecture from the Meetings tab, or just the part that matters as a voice note, then end it. The note comes back with a title, a short summary, key points and the deadlines as reminders, without you writing anything.

Can it transcribe the whole lecture?

Yes, when you record it from the Meetings tab. You get the minutes with a summary and key points, and next to them a tab with the full transcript of the lecture. If it was in Arabic and English together, both languages are kept as they were said.

Can I record a full two hour lecture?

Yes. Start it from the Meetings tab and end it when the lecture does. Recording on your iPhone is free; cloud minutes use the minutes in Pro (30 a month) or Pro+ (900 a month).

Can I take a note from a photo?

Yes. Tap the + button and choose Photo a page. Photograph up to five pages, whiteboards or screenshots, or pick them from Photos.

Is it allowed to record my lecture?

That depends on your university and your lecturer. Ask first; ActionNote does not announce itself, so the courtesy is yours.

Where do my notes go?

On your iPhone and in your own iCloud. Audio, text and pictures are sent to be organized and are not kept by us.
