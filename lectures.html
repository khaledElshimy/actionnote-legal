I was bad at taking notes.

Half the lecture came out in one language, half in another, and I spent the walk home trying to remember whether the deadline was Thursday or Sunday. My phone had a pile of voice memos I never opened once.

So ActionNote does the part I was bad at. I talk; it writes. Lock the screen, switch apps, keep listening — the recording survives, and the note comes back as a title, a short summary, key points, and the dates as reminders that actually fire.

Recording a full two-hour session end to end, with the note building as it runs, is Meeting Mode — in build for 2.0, not in the app yet. Today you capture the parts worth capturing, and the note is organized before you're out of the building.

Before you download

Can I record the whole two-hour lecture?

Not as one long session yet — that's Meeting Mode, in build for 2.0. Today ActionNote is built for shorter captures, and recording keeps going with the screen locked or while you use other apps.

Can my university see my recordings?

Nobody can. Your notes live on your iPhone and in your own iCloud — there is no ActionNote database. Audio goes to the AI once for processing and isn't kept on any server of ours.

Is 5 notes a month enough?

For a light week, yes — and it stays free forever. A full semester usually isn't, which is what Pro is for: unlimited notes at $4.99/month or $29.99/year.
