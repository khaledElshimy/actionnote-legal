Frequently asked questions

How do I record a note?

Tap the + button, then the microphone, speak, and tap again. You can also type, photograph a page, import audio or share a voice note in from WhatsApp. Each one comes back with a title, a summary and actions with reminders.

What languages are supported?

You can speak almost any language, even two in the same sentence, and the note keeps both. The app itself is in Arabic, English, Spanish and French.

What's included in Pro?

Pro organizes unlimited notes, polishes your words and includes 30 minutes of cloud meetings a month. Pro+ adds meetings with speakers, minutes and decisions, with 900 cloud minutes a month. The free plan organizes 5 notes a month, and your very first note needs no account. All plans are on the plans page.

How do I restore my subscription?

Open the upgrade screen and tap Restore. If you subscribed with the same Apple ID, your Pro access is restored.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: on your iPhone open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → ActionNote, then cancel. You'll keep Pro until the end of the current period.

How is my data handled? Is it private?

Your notes are stored on your device and, if enabled, your personal iCloud, never in a database of ours. To transcribe a recording, your audio is sent securely to our AI (Google Gemini) and isn't kept afterward. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I back up or restore my notes?

Go to Settings → Backup. iCloud backup keeps your notes safe across devices; you can also optionally include audio. Use "Restore from iCloud" on a new device.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and removes your notes from this device and your iCloud.

Can I record a whole meeting or lecture?

Yes. Open Meetings and start a meeting. Recording on your iPhone is free on every plan. Cloud minutes, with the speakers named, decisions and actions for each person, come with Pro (30 minutes a month) and Pro+ (900 minutes a month). A notification tells you when your minutes are ready.
