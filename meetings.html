The expensive meetings had no invite.

The ones that cost me were never on my calendar. The two minutes after stand-up. Someone catching me in a corridor with a favour. The client debrief in the car on the way back. Nobody takes minutes in those, because nobody scheduled them.

No bot can join a conversation that has no link. My phone was already on the table, so I made the phone do it: press record, talk it out while it's fresh, and read the follow-ups instead of trying to remember them.

Recording a full-length in-person meeting end to end, with the note building while it runs, is Meeting Mode — in build for 2.0, not in the app yet. Today ActionNote covers the capture right around the meeting, which is where the commitments actually live.

Before you download

Does it join my Zoom or Teams calls?

No, and that's deliberate. ActionNote is the phone in the room with you, for the in-person conversations a bot can't attend. If your problem is remote calls, a meeting bot is the better tool.

Can I record a full one-hour meeting?

Not as one long session yet — that's Meeting Mode, in build for 2.0. Today the pattern that works is capturing right after, or in the moments that matter, with recording that survives a locked screen.

Where does the client's confidential talk end up?

On your iPhone and in your own iCloud. There is no ActionNote database, and audio is sent to the AI once for processing rather than stored on any server of ours.
