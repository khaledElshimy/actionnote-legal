The meeting ends. The promises don't.

Everyone leaves a meeting agreeing on what happens next, and a week later nobody remembers who said they would do it. The minutes either never got written, or they got written by the one person who was also trying to talk.

So ActionNote writes the meeting minutes for you. Open Meetings, tap Start a meeting, and talk. When you end it, the recording is turned into minutes with the speakers named, what was decided, what is still open, and the actions for each person. What you promised lands in You promised, with a name and a day. What they promised lands in Others promised.

A notification tells you when your minutes are ready, even with the app closed.

Minutes of meeting template

These are the parts every good set of meeting minutes needs, and the same ones ActionNote fills in from the recording. Copy them even if you write the minutes yourself.

  1. Meeting details: title, date, time and place.
  2. Attendees: who was there and their role.
  3. Agenda: the topics discussed.
  4. Discussion: the main points on each topic, and who made them.
  5. Decisions: what was clearly agreed.
  6. Action items: each task, its owner and its due date.
  7. Open questions: what was not settled and needs follow up.
  8. Next meeting: the date, if there is one.

Before you download

How do I write meeting minutes fast?

Do not write them by hand. Put your phone on the table, start a meeting from the Meetings tab, and when you end it ActionNote writes the meeting minutes: the summary, decisions, open questions and actions for each person with their dates. The full meeting transcription stays in its own tab if you need to check something.

Does it join my Zoom or Teams calls?

No, and that is deliberate. ActionNote is the phone in the room with you, for the conversations a bot cannot attend. If your meetings are all on video calls, a meeting bot is the better tool.

Can I record a whole one hour meeting?

Yes. Start a meeting from the Meetings tab and end it when you are done. Recording on your iPhone is free; cloud minutes with speakers use the minutes in Pro (30 a month) or Pro+ (900 a month).

Where does the confidential talk end up?

Notes live on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. Audio is sent to be transcribed and organized, and is not kept by us.

Which plan do I need?

None to record on your iPhone. For cloud minutes with the speakers named, Pro gives you 30 minutes a month and Pro+ gives you 900. All plans are on the plans page.
