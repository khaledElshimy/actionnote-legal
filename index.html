Questions people ask

Where do my notes live? Can you read them?

Your notes live on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. There is no ActionNote database. Audio is sent to the AI once for processing and is not kept on any server of ours.

Which languages work?

Whatever you speak — including two languages mixed in the same sentence. Arabic/English mid-sentence switching was the original reason the app exists. The note comes back organized in the language you spoke.

Do I have to listen back to my recordings?

Never. That's the whole point. The audio stays attached to each note in case you want it, but the summary, key points, and tasks replace listening.

Does recording stop if I lock my phone?

No — recording continues with the screen locked or while you use other apps, and you get a notification when your organized note is ready.

Is there an Android or desktop version?

iPhone first (iOS 16+). A Mac companion is on the roadmap, along with long-recording support for meetings and lectures. Android depends on how iOS goes.

What happens when I cancel?

Your notes are yours — they stay on your device and iCloud. You just go back to 5 new notes a month.

Missing something? Tell me.

ActionNote is built by one person, in public, and the roadmap is genuinely shaped by users — translate mode, audio markers, and Notion export all started as user suggestions. If the app doesn't do the thing you need, I want to know.
